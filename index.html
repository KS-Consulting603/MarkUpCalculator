Foreign cost, currency, and tariff % are the only entered figures per row — everything else is calculated. The rate for each currency is fetched live from Yahoo Finance through corsproxy.io (free for a github.io URL, no key), then reduced by the rate buffer above before use — e.g. a 5% buffer on a rate of 0.88 uses 0.836, protecting against the exchange rate moving against you between quoting and paying. Tariff rates aren't published as market data the way exchange rates are, so that column stays a manual entry — use the Flexport link above to check the current number before typing it in. Nothing auto-refreshes; rates only change when you click Update Rates. Export CSV stamps the rates and margin used, for the record.
